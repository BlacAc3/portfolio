# 3D model credits

All models are from Sketchfab under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The files here are modified
copies: optimised with `pnpm models` (`scripts/optimize-models.mjs`), with
simplified meshes, WebP textures and Meshopt compression. Badge and
lettering meshes are removed from the 812 and the F12 (plus the F12's
plates and small cabin parts), and the F12 is repainted. At runtime its paint, chrome
and lights are restyled and its parts are re-rigged for the exploded view.
